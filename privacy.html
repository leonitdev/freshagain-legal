Fresh Again

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

Fresh Again ("the app") is made by Foveo LLC. This policy explains what happens to information when you or your child use the app.

The short version

  • We do not collect, sell or share personal information.
  • The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Game progress is saved only on your device.

What the app stores on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves on the device:

  • which objects you have finished and the colors you chose
  • your favorites
  • your settings (sounds, vibration, reduce motion, higher contrast, extra-easy peeling, language)
  • the state of an object you are in the middle of restoring

This information stays on the device. We never receive it.

In-app purchase

The optional full-game purchase is handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. The app only stores on the device that the full game is unlocked.

Sharing a before/after card

In the parent area, a grown-up can create an image of a finished object and open the system share sheet. The image contains only the object and its name. You choose where it goes. The app sends nothing automatically.

Permissions

The app does not use the camera, microphone, location or contacts, and it does not ask for these permissions.

Children

The app is designed for children and adults. It does not ask anyone for a name, birthdate, photo, voice, contacts or other personal information, and it contains no advertising or third-party analytics.

Deleting data

Open Settings > For grown-ups > Reset all progress, or delete the app. Either removes all game data from the device. [CONFIRM: mention OS-level device backups (iCloud / Google) if relevant]

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and post the new version on this page.
